notes & assumptions
  • synthetic lyric: 8 lines, 4–6 words each, word starts every 400ms from t=1000ms at 1x. the word holds (line 4) is fixed at 1200ms — the emphasis test. a 5s gap sits between lines 6 and 7 — the interlude test.
  • each word start fires a short oscillator blip scheduled with precise AudioContext timing. the mock clock reads audioCtx.currentTime, so clicks and the engine share one time domain.
  • word elements are the engine's span.lyrics-word nodes (in DOM order); for emphasis words the wipe --p lives on the span.lyrics-letter children, so a word counts as lit when its own --p (or its first letter's) first exceeds 0. words are expected to light in time order. a word with no --p progress 1.5s after its end is marked missed.
  • the engine highlights 40ms early by design (HIGHLIGHT_LEAD_MS), so expect deltas around −40ms. click-i / lit-i marks are taken in the same rAF loop, so deltas share about ±1 frame of quantization — comfortably inside the ±60ms pass window (highlight leading by up to 60ms is allowed).
  • seek test jumps the mock clock +20s mid-run and fires the engine's real seek path: clicks for skipped words are cancelled, the rest rescheduled; re-lock = time from the seek to the first lit word at/after the new position.
  • this page exercises the mock clock only. the AudioElementClock path still needs its own test.
  • real-browser QA is pending — this environment has no live browser. keep this tab visible during a run: rAF throttles in background tabs and invalidates the numbers.
  • run: serve the repo root over http (e.g. python3 -m http.server) and open /lyrics-lab.html, or open the deployed page. requires assets/lyrics/engine.js to exist and expose window.LyricsEngine.
